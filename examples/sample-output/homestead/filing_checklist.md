## 4. Exemptions

Eligible but not yet on file (claim these):

- **Over-65 or disabled additional school exemption** (Tax Code §11.13(c)), $60,000 exemption from school taxes: about $630 per year
  - How to claim: The district must grant it without an application if your birth date is already in its records and you have the general homestead exemption. Otherwise file Form 50-114 with the appraisal district (not the Comptroller) with a copy of your Texas driver's license or state ID showing the property address. Apply no later than the first anniversary of turning 65.
  - Documents: Texas driver's license or state ID showing the property address
  - Note: Effective January 1 of the tax year you turn 65 and applies to the whole year. An owner who is both 65 or older and disabled may choose either exemption, not both.
- **School tax ceiling (tax freeze)** (Tax Code §11.26(a)): savings not quantified
  - How to claim: There is no separate application: the ceiling follows the over-65 or disabled school exemption. Ask the school district's tax office for the dollar ceiling that applies to your account.
  - Note: Savings depend on future rate and value changes, so they are not included in the estimate.
