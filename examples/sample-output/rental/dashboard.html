Deadlines

  1. Fri, April 30, 2027Exemption application deadlineThe statute says before May 1. The chief appraiser may extend once, by written order, for good cause, by up to 60 days. Basis: Tax Code §11.43(d); §1.06.
  2. Mon, May 24, 2027Protest filing deadlineLater of May 15 and 30 days after the notice mailing date. A first-class-mail notice is presumed delivered the day it is mailed (rebuttable). A mailed protest is timely if postmarked on or before the last day. If your notice or the CAD shows an earlier date, protest by the earlier date. Basis: Tax Code §41.44(a)(1); §1.06; §1.07(c); §1.08.
  3. Tue, July 20, 2027ARB records-approval date (not an owner deadline)The ARB must approve the appraisal records by this date. A protest filed after your deadline can still be heard, if the ARB finds good cause, only until the ARB actually approves the records, which may be earlier. Do not treat this as your filing date. A county of 1,000,000 or more may postpone approval to August 30 by CAD board resolution. Basis: Tax Code §41.44(b); §41.12(a), (c).
  4. Tue, February 1, 2028Taxes become delinquentTax is delinquent if not paid before February 1, so the last day to pay without penalty is January 31. If the bill is mailed after January 10, the date is the first day of the next month that gives at least 21 days after the mailing date. Read the date on your bill. Basis: Tax Code §31.02(a); §31.03; §31.04(a).
