# Texas property tax protest: verified law and figures

**Verified as of 2026-10-08. Applies to tax years 2025 and later unless a row says otherwise. Written for the 2027 protest season (tax year 2027: notices April 2027, protest deadline May 2027).**

Nothing here was taken from v1 on trust. Every row was checked against the statute text, the Texas Constitution text, a Comptroller or SOAH page, or a bill page.

## How to read this file

- **Statute text** was read on 2026-10-08 from the Texas Legislative Council server, which feeds statutes.capitol.texas.gov. The Tax Code files on that server carry build dates from 2026-04-10 to 2026-09-01 and already include the 89th Legislature (2025) acts.
- **Scripted fetching:** statutes.capitol.texas.gov draws the text with JavaScript, so a plain HTTP fetch returns an empty shell. The same official text is served as static HTML at `https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/TX/htm/TX.<chapter>.htm` (also `CN/htm/CN.8.htm`, `GV/htm/GV.2003.htm`). The Source URL column gives the statutes.capitol.texas.gov page; swap the host and path for scripts.
- **Confidence tags**
  - `verified-primary`: read on a statutes.capitol.texas.gov / Legislative Council page, comptroller.texas.gov, soah.texas.gov, or capitol.texas.gov.
  - `derived`: a date or example I computed from a verified-primary rule. The rule is cited in the row.
  - `secondary-only`: seen only on a news site, law firm, or CAD page. Never stated as fact here. These are listed in "Unresolved / needs human check".
- "As-of" is the date I read the source. Where a row depends on a law that can still change, the "Tax years" column says so.
- Section 41.41(a) has a second text version that starts 2027-01-01 (it drops the circuit-breaker ground). The 2027 version is the one that applies to the 2027 protest season. See the note in section 5.

Short names used below: **CAD** appraisal district; **ARB** appraisal review board; **Const.** Texas Constitution; **PTAD** Comptroller Property Tax Assistance Division.

## 10. Changes from v1

Every v1 claim that was wrong, stale, or incomplete, and the correct fact:

1. **School homestead exemption "$100,000 (as of 2023 amendment)"** is stale. It is **$140,000** for tax years 2025 and later (§11.13(b); Const. art. VIII §1-b(c)).
2. **Over-65/disabled additional exemption "$10,000"** is stale. It is **$60,000** for 2025 and later (§11.13(c)). With the general exemption, $200,000.
3. **Homestead requirements "driver's license + vehicle registration"**: only a copy of the Texas driver's license or state ID with the property address is required (§11.43(j)); registration is not.
4. **"Can be filed any time; applies to Jan 1 of the year filed"**: the deadline is before May 1 of the tax year (§11.43(d)); late filing is accepted up to 2 years after the delinquency date (§11.431). Qualification is judged on January 1 (§11.42), with the new-buyer and turned-65 exceptions.
5. **"10% annual cap on appraisal increases"** needs the mechanics: lesser-of rule, starts the January 1 after the first exempt year, adds new improvements, ends when no qualifying owner/spouse remains (§23.23).
6. **Disabled veteran "100% = TOTAL exemption, $0 property taxes"** is wrong as written. §11.22 tops out at $12,000. The total exemption is §11.131(b) and covers the **residence homestead only** (100% VA compensation and a 100% or IU rating).
7. **Disabled veteran section omitted** the $12,000 rule for age 65+/blind/limb-loss (§11.22(b)), the donated-home exemption (§11.132), and the survivor exemptions (§11.131(c), §11.133, §11.134, and new §11.136 for tax year 2026+).
8. **"Surviving spouse ... (§11.22(h))"** is the wrong subsection. It is §11.22(c) (and (d) for active-duty deaths).
9. **Required documents "VA letter, DD-214, TX driver's license"**: Form 50-135 asks for a driver's license/state ID copy and VA or service-branch proof of the current rating; no DD-214.
10. **School tax ceiling "can never increase above the amount in the year you turned 65"** is incomplete: since 2024 the ceiling is adjusted down for rate compression and exemption increases and up for new improvements (§11.26(a-10), (b)); portability needs a CAD certificate (§11.26(h)); local units have their own optional ceilings (§11.261).
11. **Protest ground "market value (§41.43(b)(1))"** is wrong. The ground is **§41.41(a)(1)**. §41.43(b)(1) is one of three tests the CAD uses to defeat an *unequal appraisal* claim.
12. **Protest ground "unequal appraisal (§41.43(b)(3))"**: the ground is **§41.41(a)(2)**. §41.43(b)(3) (median appraised value of adjusted comparables) is the comparables-based test within it; the court counterpart is §42.26(a)(3).
13. **Burden of proof was not mentioned.** The CAD carries the burden by a preponderance (§41.43(a)); clear and convincing in two owner-triggered cases (§41.43(a-1), (a-3)); the ARB cannot raise the value (§41.47(c-2)).
14. **"Appraisal notices mailed mid-April"**: the statute says by April 1 for homestead and May 1 for other property, "or as soon thereafter as practicable," and only when the value rose or another listed event occurred (§25.19(a)).
15. **"Protest deadline May 15 (or 30 days after the notice date, whichever later)"** is right in substance. Add: the 30 days run from delivery (mail presumed delivered on mailing), and a weekend/holiday last day rolls forward (§1.06). **For 2027, May 15 is a Saturday, so the deadline is Monday May 17, 2027.**
16. **"Informal hearings May-June; formal June-August"** is not statutory. The ARB must approve records by July 20 (§41.12); the informal conference is a right on request (§41.445).
17. **"Late protest ... (errors, late notice delivery, etc.)"** was vague. Real doors: good cause before the ARB approves the records (§41.44(b)); no-notice protest before delinquency (§41.411, §41.44(c)); motion to correct (§25.25).
18. **"~85% of protests settle informally"** has no official source. Remove or label as unverified.
19. **"Formal ARB hearing: 3-person citizen panel ... ~15 minutes ... bring 4 printed copies ... the panel votes"**: panels are 3 or more members or a single member on request; the full board decides from the panel's recommendation (§41.45(d), (b-4), (d-2)). Time limits and copy counts are set by each ARB's hearing procedures, not by statute; exchange rules are §41.45(h) and 34 TAC §9.805.
20. **"The process is free and low-risk"** is supported: no fee (§41.41(d)), and the ARB cannot raise the value above the CAD's without the owner's agreement (§41.47(c-2)).
21. **Form 50-132 pointer** is incomplete: there are two forms (50-132 for counties over 120,000, 50-132-A for smaller), the PDFs live at `comptroller.texas.gov/forms/<number>.pdf`, and the form is optional (§41.44(d)).
22. **No post-ARB remedies, no evidence-exchange rules, no postponement or remote-hearing rights** in v1. Now covered in sections 6 and 8, `deadlines.md`, and `beyond-arb.md`.
23. **The "Texas CAD table" phone numbers** were not re-verified here (the counties agent owns that).
